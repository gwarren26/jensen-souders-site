Our Story

Over a Century of Chemical Warehousing Excellence

Jensen-Souders & Associates, Inc. traces its roots to 1920, when the Fred A. Jensen Company opened its doors at 510 N. Dearborn in Chicago, Illinois. What began as a modest chemical distribution operation grew steadily through the decades, incorporating in Illinois in 1960 and formally becoming Jensen-Souders & Associates, Inc. in 1966 when Wilson Souders joined as a partner.

In 1969 the company relocated to 725 N. Baker Drive in Itasca, IL — a move that set the stage for decades of facility growth. From an original 42,000 sq. ft. warehouse to today's 200,000+ sq. ft. across two locations, every expansion has been driven by one goal: serving our clients better.

Through more than 100 years and multiple generations of leadership, our core values have never changed: safety, compliance, reliability, and the kind of personal service that only a family-operated business can provide.

Our History

More Than a Century in the Making

1920

Fred A. Jensen Company Founded

Established at 510 N. Dearborn, Chicago, IL — the beginning of a century-long legacy in chemical warehousing.

1960

Incorporated in Illinois

Formally incorporated in the State of Illinois, providing a stable legal foundation for continued growth.

1966

Name Changed to Jensen-Souders & Associates, Inc.

  • Wilson Souders attains shares in the company, bringing new energy and partnership to the operation.
1969

Moved to Itasca, IL

  • Relocated to 725 N. Baker Drive, Itasca, IL
  • Original warehouse: 42,000 sq. ft.
  • Primarily floor-stacked storage configuration
1981

First Major Expansion

Added 18,000 sq. ft. to the Itasca warehouse, bringing total capacity to 60,000 sq. ft.

1997

Purchased 749 N. Baker Drive

Acquired the adjacent 43,000 sq. ft. building at 749 N. Baker Drive, dramatically expanding storage capacity.

1998

Flammable Storage & Racking Added

Dedicated flammable storage room constructed and racking systems installed — enabling safer, more efficient product organization.

2003

Hazardous Storage Facility Conversion

749 N. Baker Drive converted into a dedicated hazardous materials storage facility, fully compliant with DOT, EPA, and OSHA standards.

2023

Second Warehouse Opened

New 60,000 sq. ft. facility opened at 3915 Morriem Drive, Belvidere, IL — expanding capacity and geographic reach to better serve clients across the region.

Today

200,000+ Sq. Ft. & Growing

Two modern facilities with a combined capacity over 200,000 sq. ft., operated by a dedicated team committed to the same values that have guided us since 1920.
