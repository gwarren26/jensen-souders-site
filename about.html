About Jensen-Souders & Associates

Over four decades of trusted chemical warehousing and logistics in the Chicago metropolitan area.

Our Story

Built on Integrity, Grown by Trust

Jensen-Souders & Associates, Inc. was founded in the Chicago area with a straightforward mission: to provide chemical manufacturers and distributors with a warehousing partner they could genuinely depend on — one that treats their products, their customers, and their compliance requirements with the same care they would.

From our earliest days as a regional chemical storage provider to our current position as a full-service logistics company, our core values have never wavered: safety, compliance, reliability, and exceptional customer service.

Today we operate a modern, expansive warehouse complex in the Chicago metropolitan area, purpose-built for chemical handling with all necessary safety systems, segregation capabilities, and regulatory approvals in place.

Our Facility

Purpose-Built for Chemical Logistics

Our Chicago-area warehouse facility is purpose-engineered for the specific demands of chemical storage and distribution. Situated in a prime logistics corridor with excellent access to major interstate highways, rail intermodals, and O'Hare International Airport, we are ideally positioned to serve Midwest and national supply chains.

  • Extensive square footage with room for bulk chemical storage
  • Dedicated hazmat segregation bays with secondary containment
  • Full-coverage fire detection and suppression systems
  • Temperature-monitored storage zones
  • Forklift-served dock doors with truck courts for easy maneuvering
  • 24/7 security monitoring and access control
  • On-site scale for accurate weight certification
