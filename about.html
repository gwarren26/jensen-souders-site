Who We Are

Over a Century of Expertise

We provide warehousing services for chemicals, rubber products, and other miscellaneous products. Services include storage, inventory control, and direct shipment to your customers from our warehouse. We have trained personnel for hazardous as well as non-hazardous material.

We follow the guidelines of ACD Responsible Distribution Process (RDP), similar to ISO 9000 standards. Jensen-Souders is a charter member of the ACD Affiliate program and has been a chemical handling facility for over 80 years, both as a distributor and as a public warehouse.

Our History

Background Information

1920

Fred A. Jensen Company founded at 510 N. Dearborn, Chicago, IL.

1960

Incorporated in the State of Illinois.

1966

Renamed Jensen-Souders & Assoc., Inc.; Wilson Souders attains shares.

1969

Relocated to 725 N. Baker Drive, Itasca, IL. Original warehouse 42,000 sq. ft.; primarily floor-stacked storage.

1981

Added 18,000 sq. ft.

1997

Purchased 749 N. Baker Drive, 43,000 sq. ft.

1998

Flammable storage room and racking systems added.

2003

749 N. Baker Drive converted to hazardous storage facility.

2023

Second warehouse opened at 3915 Morriem Drive, Belvidere, IL; originally 30,000 sq. ft., now 60,000 sq. ft.

Present building capacity — 140,000 sq. ft. in Itasca (horseshoe layout with center parking), fully sprinklered. 60,000 sq. ft. in Belvidere.
