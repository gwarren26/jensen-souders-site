DOT Compliance

Every Shipment. Every Regulation.

Federal regulations governing the transportation of hazardous materials are extensive and complex. A single error in classification, packaging, marking, labeling, or documentation can result in costly fines, shipment delays, and — more importantly — real safety risks.

Our hazmat shipping team is trained and certified to navigate these regulations confidently. We verify correct UN numbers and hazard classes, proper packaging groups, required placard types, emergency response phone numbers, and all documentation requirements before any hazmat shipment leaves our dock.

  • Correct DOT hazard classification verification
  • UN packaging group assignment and packaging compliance
  • Proper marking, labeling, and placarding
  • Emergency response phone number compliance
  • Carrier certification and approval verification
  • International shipping (IATA/IMDG) coordination available
