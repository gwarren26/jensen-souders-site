Our Facilities

Industrial Warehouses

Jensen-Souders has two public flammable, hazmat, and chemical warehouse facilities offering a total of 240,000 square feet of warehouse space. Our main facility boasts 140,000 square feet, including four hazardous materials rooms with a separate area for flammable storage, as well as bulk storage for non-hazardous materials.

Located in Northern Illinois, less than one mile from major highways and five miles from O'Hare International Airport. Our second facility offers 100,000 square feet and is located in Belvidere, Illinois.
