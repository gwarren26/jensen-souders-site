Who We Are

Chicago's Premier Chemical Warehouse Partner

Founded in 1920 as the Fred A. Jensen Company in Chicago, Jensen-Souders & Associates, Inc. has built a century-long reputation as a trusted partner for chemical manufacturers, distributors, and end-users who demand safe, compliant, and cost-effective warehousing solutions.

As a proud member of the National Association of Chemical Distributors (NACD), we uphold the highest standards of Responsible Distribution® — ensuring every shipment, every pallet, and every drum is handled with precision and care.

  • NACD Responsible Distribution® member
  • DOT & EPA compliant hazmat operations
  • Modern fire-suppression and containment systems
  • Climate-controlled storage zones available
  • 24/7 secured facility with full inventory tracking

Commitment to Safety

NACD Responsible Distribution® Member

Jensen-Souders & Associates is a certified member of the National Association of Chemical Distributors (NACD) and committed to the Responsible Distribution® Code of Management Practices. This third-party verified program ensures our operations meet the highest environmental, health, safety, and security standards in the industry.

Our compliance program covers product stewardship, emergency response planning, employee training, and continuous performance improvement — protecting our customers, our community, and the environment.
