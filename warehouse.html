Our Facilities

Industrial, Hazmat, Flammable & Chemical Warehouse

Jensen-Souders utilizes five connected secured warehouses for industrial, hazmat, flammable, and chemical storage. We are monitored 24 hours a day by our security company, as well as the Itasca police and fire department. Our location in an industrial park in the western suburbs of Chicago is safe and secure, featuring a low crime rate and controlled access both inside and outside the warehouse.

We have nine docks to load and unload incoming trailers and containers, as well as a street-level entrance to load small vehicles. We have several warehouses with sprinklered racking and one warehouse devoted exclusively to flammable material. Jensen-Souders uses only EE forklifts for the safe handling of hazardous material. All employees are fully trained for the safe handling of hazardous and non-hazardous material.

We have 10,000 sq. ft. of 80°F heated storage. The Hot Room, as well as the rest of the warehouse, is fully sprinklered and includes both racking space and bulk storage. Temperature is maintained at a constant 80°F year-round.
