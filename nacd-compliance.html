Our Commitment

NACD Responsible Distribution®

Jensen-Souders & Associates is a proud member of the National Association of Chemical Distributors (NACD) and an active participant in the Responsible Distribution® program — the chemical distribution industry's voluntary, third-party verified environmental, health, safety, and security initiative.

Responsible Distribution® requires members to continuously improve their performance across six comprehensive Code of Management Practices: Community Awareness and Emergency Response, Pollution Prevention, Process Safety, Distribution, Employee Health and Safety, and Product Stewardship.

Membership is not automatic — it requires documented compliance with all program requirements and is verified through independent third-party audits conducted on a regular basis. We are proud of our clean audit record and our ongoing commitment to continuous improvement.

Code of Management Practices

Six Pillars of Responsible Distribution®

Our operations are structured around all six NACD Code of Management Practices, each supported by written procedures, training programs, and performance metrics.

🚨

Community Awareness & Emergency Response

We maintain a current emergency response plan, provide required community notification, and coordinate with local emergency responders. Our team participates in CHEMTREC and maintains 24-hour emergency contact capability.

🌿

Pollution Prevention

We actively work to reduce waste generation, minimize environmental releases, and improve the environmental profile of our operations through ongoing process improvements and waste reduction initiatives.

⚙️

Process Safety

Written operating procedures, management of change protocols, pre-startup safety reviews, and regular process hazard analyses ensure our chemical handling processes are inherently safe by design.

🚛

Distribution

Our distribution practices encompass carrier qualification, hazmat shipping compliance, route risk assessment, and customer product stewardship — ensuring safe movement of chemicals from our dock to the customer's door.

👷

Employee Health & Safety

Comprehensive safety training, regular drills, PPE programs, industrial hygiene monitoring, and incident investigation systems protect our most valuable asset — our people — every single day.

🧪

Product Stewardship

We actively manage the safe use, handling, and disposal of the products we distribute — providing SDS sheets, safe handling guidance, and responsible disposal information to all customers.

Regulatory Compliance

Compliant Across All Applicable Regulations

🚛 DOT – Department of Transportation

Full compliance with 49 CFR Parts 100-185 governing hazardous materials transportation. All outbound chemical shipments are classified, packaged, marked, labeled, placarded, and documented per current DOT regulations. All employees involved in hazmat transportation are trained per 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

🌿 EPA – Environmental Protection Agency

Compliance with RCRA for any hazardous waste generated by our operations, Tier II reporting under EPCRA Section 312, and SPCC (Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure) planning for facilities with sufficient oil storage capacity.

👷 OSHA – Occupational Safety and Health Administration

Compliance with 29 CFR 1910.119 (PSM for highly hazardous chemicals where applicable), 1910.120 (HAZWOPER), 1910.1200 (HazCom/GHS), and all other applicable general industry standards. Regular safety inspections, training documentation, and incident recordkeeping per OSHA requirements.

🔥 NFPA – National Fire Protection Association

Facility design and operations comply with NFPA 30 (Flammable and Combustible Liquids Code), NFPA 13 (Sprinkler Systems), and other applicable NFPA standards. Regular fire protection system inspections and testing per NFPA schedules.

Common Questions

Compliance FAQ

What is the NACD Responsible Distribution® program? ▼

Responsible Distribution® is the National Association of Chemical Distributors' voluntary environmental, health, safety, and security initiative. Member companies commit to continuously improving their performance and must undergo third-party verification audits to maintain their membership in good standing. It is widely recognized as the gold standard for safety and compliance in the chemical distribution industry.

How does Jensen-Souders handle regulatory changes? ▼

Our compliance team actively monitors regulatory updates from DOT, EPA, OSHA, the State of Illinois, and local authorities on an ongoing basis. We subscribe to regulatory update services, participate in NACD compliance webinars, and conduct regular internal audits to identify and close any gaps. When regulations change, we update our procedures and training promptly.

What training do Jensen-Souders employees receive? ▼

All employees receive job-specific safety training before beginning work in chemical handling areas. Hazmat warehouse staff hold OSHA HAZWOPER certification with annual refreshers. Employees involved in shipping chemical products receive DOT hazmat employee training with triennial recertification. All employees participate in annual emergency response drills and HazCom training.

Can Jensen-Souders handle my specific chemical products? ▼

We handle a wide range of industrial chemicals across multiple DOT hazard classes. Before accepting any new product, our team reviews the Safety Data Sheet (SDS), compatibility with existing stored materials, and any special handling requirements. Contact our team with your specific product information and we'll provide a clear answer and any applicable conditions.

Does Jensen-Souders have a SPCC plan? ▼

Yes. We maintain a current Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) plan as required by EPA regulations. The plan is reviewed and updated at least every five years, or whenever a material change occurs in our facility or operations. Our containment systems are designed and maintained to prevent any releases from reaching navigable waters.
