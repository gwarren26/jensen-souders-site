Hazmat Expertise

Certified Hazardous Materials Storage

Storing hazardous materials requires more than just space — it requires specialized infrastructure, rigorously trained personnel, meticulous documentation, and an unwavering commitment to regulatory compliance. Jensen-Souders & Associates delivers all of the above.

Our dedicated hazmat warehouse areas are designed specifically for flammables, corrosives, oxidizers, and other regulated substances. Strict segregation keeps incompatible materials apart, while our secondary containment systems and emergency response protocols ensure we're prepared for any situation.

  • DOT hazard class segregation per 49 CFR
  • Full secondary containment sumps in all hazmat areas
  • Explosion-proof electrical systems in flammable storage
  • Automatic fire suppression with NFPA compliance
  • Continuous air monitoring and ventilation control
  • HAZWOPER-trained warehouse personnel
  • 24-hour emergency response capability

Materials We Handle

DOT Hazard Classes Accepted

Our facility is configured to safely store a broad range of hazardous materials across multiple DOT hazard classifications.

🔥

Flammable Liquids (Class 3)

Explosion-proof electrical, grounding and bonding systems, dedicated ventilation, and fire suppression calibrated for flammable liquids storage.

⚗️

Corrosives (Class 8)

Acid- and alkali-resistant containment, appropriate personal protective equipment stations, and separate storage zones for acids and bases.

🧪

Toxic Substances (Class 6)

Enhanced access control, additional PPE requirements, and heightened documentation protocols for toxic and poison materials.

💨

Flammable Gases (Class 2)

Compressed and liquefied gas cylinder storage with proper restraint, segregated ventilated areas, and gas detection monitoring.

⚡

Oxidizers (Class 5)

Isolated storage zones that keep oxidizers away from flammables and combustibles, with dedicated containment and ventilation systems.

🌍

Environmentally Hazardous

EPA-compliant storage for RCRA-regulated materials, with appropriate manifesting, tracking, and disposal coordination as required.

Safety Infrastructure

Multiple Layers of Protection

Our approach to hazmat safety is based on defense-in-depth — multiple independent systems working together to prevent incidents and minimize consequences in the event of any release.

  • Passive secondary containment sized to 110% of largest container
  • NFPA 30-compliant fire suppression with foam application capability
  • Continuous combustible gas and toxic vapor monitoring
  • High-volume exhaust ventilation with makeup air systems
  • Emergency eyewash and safety shower stations throughout facility
  • On-site spill response kits calibrated for stored materials
  • Written emergency response plan with fire department pre-planning
  • OSHA-required hazard communication and SDS management
